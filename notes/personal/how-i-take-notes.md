# How I take notes (and why this site exists)

This site started life as a portfolio for recruiters. It's becoming something more useful to me day-to-day: a **public second brain**.

## The loop

1. **Capture** — anything interesting goes into a quick note. Friction is the enemy.
2. **Connect** — link notes to each other with `[[wiki-links]]` so ideas cluster instead of scatter.
3. **Curate** — periodically clean up, merge, and promote the good stuff.

## Why in public?

- Writing for an audience forces clarity.
- "Learn in public" turns notes into a portfolio as a side effect.
- Future me is the most frequent reader anyway.

## The rule

> Everything here is public and curated. If it's not something I'd be happy for anyone to read, it doesn't go in the repo.

That single rule keeps a public wiki and a personal brain from fighting each other.
