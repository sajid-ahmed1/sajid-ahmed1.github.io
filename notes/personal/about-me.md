# About Me

I'm a data economist and analyst at Vodafone Group UK, currently working as a Channels Insight Analyst and Product Owner in Paddington. I've been at Vodafone since 2021, starting on the Technology Discover graduate scheme and rotating through data science, internal audit analytics, and business insights before landing in my current role.

## What I do day to day

I led the design and delivery of a heatmap tool from concept to production that brings commercial teams closer to actionable data. I spend a lot of time automating things that shouldn't be manual, like cutting end-of-month reporting time by 70% through Tableau restructuring, and building an automated issue-reporting system that killed 90% of the back-and-forth email traffic on web KPIs.

Before that I was building BigQuery pipelines handling over 1TB of real-time data, designing CI/CD frameworks with Apache Airflow for automated ML model training, and running statistical modelling to cut API errors by 50%.

## Education

A-Levels were Further Maths (A*), Maths (A*), Economics (B) at the London Academy of Excellence.
