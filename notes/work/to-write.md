## Medium: Breaking asymmetric information in the employment market

The idea: employers hold all the structural power in a job market because they know more about what a role actually involves, what the salary band really is, and how candidates will be evaluated — while candidates have to signal value with imperfect proxies (CVs, degrees, interviews). The article would explore how this asymmetry can be disrupted from the candidate's side.
